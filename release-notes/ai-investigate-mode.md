## Changes

- Ask AI has an optional Investigate mode. The AI can ask for read-only commands on the connected machine. You approve each command, review the output, and choose whether to send it back. The Suggest mode is unchanged.
- Administrators turn Investigate on in the AI settings, with a per-command timeout. The audit log has a new AI group.

## After updating

- No action needed. Investigate mode is off until an administrator enables it and accepts the data-sharing notice.
