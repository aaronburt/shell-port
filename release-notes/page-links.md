## Changes

- Links can open the Account tab with `?page=account` and the Audit log with `?page=audit`. They work alone or beside host tabs, such as `?open=web1&page=account`.
- The address bar follows the Account and Audit log tabs as they open and close, the same as host tabs.
- Audit log links are ignored for users who are not admins.
