## Changes

- Ask AI now opens as a drawer on the right instead of a panel under the terminal, so the terminal keeps its full height. On screens 1400 px or wider it replaces the session panel instead of covering the terminal.
- Drag the drawer's left edge to resize it. The width is remembered in this browser.
- Questions and answers stay in the drawer as a conversation until you press New. Each answer has its own Copy and Insert buttons, and Insert always goes to the terminal the question was asked about, with a note if you have focused another pane since.
- Closing the drawer no longer stops an Investigate run. The sparkle button shows a badge while a command is waiting for your approval. Press Stop to end a run.
- The context options are behind a Context chip, and an empty drawer suggests example questions for the host's operating system.
